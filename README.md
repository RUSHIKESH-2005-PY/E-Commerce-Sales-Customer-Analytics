**# E-Commerce Sales \& Customer Analytics**



**## Project Overview**



**This is an end-to-end e-commerce analytics project focused on analyzing sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and business trends.**



**The project covers data preparation, SQL analysis, Python exploratory data analysis, customer segmentation, dashboard development, testing, and deployment.**



**## Business Objectives**



**- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance**

**- Identify top-performing products and categories**

**- Analyze customer purchasing behavior**

**- Identify high-value customers**

**- Analyze regional sales performance**

**- Identify sales trends and patterns**

**- Perform customer segmentation**

**- Build an interactive business dashboard**

**- Generate actionable business recommendations**
